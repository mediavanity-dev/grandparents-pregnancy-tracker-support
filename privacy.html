Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Grandparents Pregnancy Tracker collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests.

What you enter

The grandbabies, due dates, journal notes, photos and checklist ticks you enter are stored on your phone only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your phone (iCloud or a computer for iPhone, Google's backup for Android), they are included in that backup under your own account.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a journal entry. The photo is saved inside the app on your phone and is not shared.

Email

If you email support, your address and message are used only to reply to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
