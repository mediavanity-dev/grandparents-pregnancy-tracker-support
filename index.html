This app shares general information for grandparents and is not medical advice. Every pregnancy is different; the parents' doctor or midwife is the person to ask.

Common questions

How does the app work out which week it is?

From the due date. A pregnancy is counted as 40 weeks, so the app counts back 40 weeks from the due date and shows how many weeks and days along it is today. If the parents' due date changes after a scan, update it and everything moves with it.

How do I change the due date or the baby's name?

Tap the gear icon at the top of Today to open Settings, then tap Edit next to the grandbaby.

Can I follow more than one grandbaby, or twins?

Yes. In Settings, tap Add another grandbaby. Switch between them with the name at the top of Today. For twins, tick Expecting twins when you add or edit the grandbaby.

The baby has arrived! What now?

Congratulations! In Settings, tap Edit and fill in Born on. Today then shows the birthday and how many days old the baby is, and the journal stays with them.

How do I add a photo to the journal?

In Journal, tap New entry, then the photo field. You can take a new photo or choose one from your library. Photos are made smaller to save space and stay on your iPhone.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. In Settings, under Text size, choose one of the three sizes.

Where is everything kept, and is it backed up?

On your iPhone only. If you back up the phone to iCloud or a computer, the app's information is part of that backup. There is no account, and nothing is sent to us.
